Get To Know Me

My Interests

I enjoy watching movies, Korean dramas, and anime, especially stories with strong character development, romance, and slice-of-life moments. Outside of screen time, I like playing different kinds of video games whenever I have free time to unwind and challenge myself.

Being in the TVL - ICT Computer Programming strand also makes me curious about how websites and apps work behind the scenes, so I like experimenting with layouts and code.

My Personality

I'm usually quiet and observant around people I don't know well yet, taking a little time to warm up in new environments. Once I get comfortable with friends or classmates, though, I become much more talkative and energetic.

I value loyalty, good humor, and getting things done—and yes, I can be confident (maybe a little too confident sometimes!).

My Goals in Life

My main short-term goal is to finish Senior High School strong, graduate from college, and build a stable, rewarding career in tech or programming.

More importantly, one of my biggest dreams is to give back to my parents for all their sacrifices and hard work—including helping them reach a point where they can take things easy and retire comfortably.

Cooking for Fun

Aside from watching shows and playing games, I enjoy cooking during my free time. Trying out different recipes and preparing food is a relaxing break from staring at a screen.

I like learning how simple ingredients come together and making dishes that family and friends can enjoy.
